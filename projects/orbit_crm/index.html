    My pages tutorial

    Learn how to create and manage your own CRM pages.

    add_circle 1. Create a page
    1. Click the + button beside My pages.
    2. Enter a page title, for example Finance.
    3. Choose the page type: Table or Kanban.
    4. Add your fields, then click Create page.
    table 2. Using a Table page
    • Use Add new to create a record and fill in its fields.
    • Each record appears as a row in the table.
    • Use the Actions column to edit or delete an individual record.
    • Use the search box and pagination to find records.
    view_kanban 3. Using a Kanban page
    • Create at least 3 sections, such as To do, In progress, and Completed.
    • Use Add new to create an item. New items start in the first section.
    • Drag and drop cards between sections to change their stage.
    • Choose up to 3 fields with Show on Kanban item to display on each card. Other fields remain available in Add/Edit.
    • Use Edit on a card to update its details.
    tune 4. Fields and page settings

    Available field types: Input, Selector, Date, Currency, URL, Email, and Textarea. Selector fields can have your own comma-separated options. Mark a field as Required when it must be filled in.

    Use Edit page to change the page title, fields, Kanban sections, and card display fields. Use Delete page to remove the entire page and all records inside it.

    infoYour custom pages and records are saved automatically in this browser's local CRM database. Export a JSON backup regularly to keep a separate copy.

    Create a new page

    Create a new page with its own table and custom input fields.

    Fields

    Add the fields for each record. For Kanban, choose up to 3 fields to show on each card.

    Kanban sections

    Create at least 3 columns. You can add or remove sections.

    Example: To do, In progress, Completed

    infoThese fields will appear on each Kanban card and in its Add/Edit form. The sections above control the Kanban columns.

    How to use Tasks

    A quick guide to organizing and moving your tasks.

    mouse

    1. Scroll around the board

    Hold the right mouse button and drag left or right to move across task columns. Drag up or down to scroll the page vertically.

    drag_indicator

    2. Move a task to another status

    Hold the left mouse button on a task card and drag it to To do, In progress, Completed, or Failed. Drop it in the column to update its status.

    save

    3. Changes save automatically

    When you drop a task, its new status is saved automatically and the column count updates.

    Tip: Select Edit on a task card to change its details. Use the search field to find a task.

    How to use Sales Pipeline

    A quick guide to moving around and managing your deals.

    mouse

    1. Scroll around the board

    Hold the right mouse button, then drag left or right to move across pipeline columns. Drag up or down to scroll the page vertically. Release the button when you're done.

    drag_indicator

    2. Move a deal to another stage

    Use the left mouse button to click and hold a deal card, then drag it into Prospecting, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, or Lost. Drop it inside the column to update its stage.

    save

    3. Changes save automatically

    After you drop a deal, its new stage is saved in IndexedDB automatically. The column count updates too.

    Tip: Click Edit on a deal card when you want to change its title, value, company, or expected close date.

    Storage limit reached

    Your new record was not saved. Your existing data is still there.

    The CRM has reached the storage limit configured in Settings.

    What you can do

    • Open Settings and increase the app limit, up to 10 GB.
    • Export a JSON backup before moving or cleaning up data.
    • If your browser or PC is low on space, free up disk space on your PC and try again.

    The app limit cannot exceed the storage your browser and PC make available. Increasing this setting does not create extra disk space.
